Built for where YC companies actually end up.

Multi-entity register

One register, every entity. Not three accounts.

A few platforms support multiple entities. Fewer support multi-currency grants, and some countries aren't covered at all. EquityList supports all three, in one register.

Multi-currency grants

Multi-currency, multi-class. In one place.

A grant in INR and a grant in USD, across different share classes, don't need separate systems to track. They live in the same register, valued and reported together.

Cross-entity reporting

Cross-entity reporting. No manual exports.

The consolidated view across every entity is generated straight from the live register, not assembled by hand from exports pulled out of three separate accounts.

Frequently asked questions.

If your question isn't here, ask a person instead, we'll get back to you fast.

Ask a question
Who qualifies for the 50%?

Any Y Combinator company, any batch. Mention your batch when you sign up or book a demo.

Do I have to be switching from another platform?

No. It applies whether you are coming from Pulley, Carta, another platform, or setting up your first cap table from a spreadsheet.

What happens in year two?

You move to standard pricing, which is published on our pricing page. Compare it before you sign anything, with us or with anyone else.

How long does migration actually take?

Self-serve, most cap tables are live in under two hours. Assisted, we aim to have you live within 12 hours of receiving your export and account details. Complex structures take longer and we tell you upfront rather than letting the clock slip.

I am on Pulley. What do I need to know?

Pulley ceases operations on December 8, 2026, and the Carta opt-in closes November 30. Pulley will not assist a migration to anyone but Carta, so export your data while the account is live. Our full Pulley guide covers what to pull and in what order.

Do we have to re-execute our existing grants and agreements?

No. Existing grants, agreements and consents move across as historical records. Nothing is re-papered or re-signed.

Is setup free?

Yes. Migration support is included on every plan, with no separate fee.

Is EquityList a Y Combinator partner?

No. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by Y Combinator. This is a discount we offer to YC companies.
