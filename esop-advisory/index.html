Frequently asked questions.

How long does it take to create the scheme?

Once we've received all necessary data and supporting documents, we ordinarily require up to 4 working days to create the scheme thoroughly and accurately.

How many iterations/changes are supported?

We offer 2-3 rounds of revisions to refine the scheme and ensure it fully meets your requirements. Additional changes may be considered on a case-by-case basis.

Which geographies do we cover?

We currently support equity plans for companies operating in the US, India, Singapore, UAE.

What should be my equity pool size?

Your equity pool should strike a balance between incentivizing talent and minimizing dilution. We analyze your hiring plans, funding roadmap, and industry benchmarks to recommend an optimal pool size.

How many units should I give to each employee?

We help you create a fair and competitive allocation strategy based on factors like seniority, role impact, and market standards. Our unit modeling ensures employees receive meaningful equity while maintaining the company's long-term sustainability.

Does EquityList help with the reporting associated with equity plans?

Yes. Our platform generates all necessary reports in real time at no additional cost. (Please note: access to the platform is separate from our advisory services.) We provide ESOP expense reporting compliant with:

  • ASC 718 (US GAAP) for the US
  • IND AS 15 and 102 for India
  • IFRS disclosure reports applicable to both

Cap table updates, including a fully diluted cap table. Ongoing tracking reports, such as:

  • ESOP/SAR vesting reports
  • Exercise pending allotment reports
Does EquityList offer option valuation using Black-Scholes?

Yes, EquityList provides option valuation using the Black-Scholes method, reach out to us to learn more.

Do I need to create a trust to have an equity plan?

It is not mandatory to set up a trust to run an equity plan. Many companies grant equity awards without one; however, some choose to establish a trust to streamline share transfers and simplify administration.
