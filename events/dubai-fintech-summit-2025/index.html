About This Scorecard

This Equity Management Health Scorecard evaluates four critical aspects of your company's equity management practices:

  • Cap Table Complexity: Measures how simple or complex your cap table structure is. Lower complexity generally indicates easier management.
  • Transparency Score: Evaluates how visible and accessible your equity information is to stakeholders.
  • Fundraising Readiness: Assesses how prepared your equity structure is for your next funding round.
  • ROI Potential: Estimates the potential return on investment in improving your equity management practices.

Use these scores to identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions about your equity management strategy.
