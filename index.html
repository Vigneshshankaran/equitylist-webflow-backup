Your ownership ledger is the record,the intelligence sits on top.

Traditional cap table tools maintain a register, store grant records, and log valuations. Today, this reconciliation is a person downloading reports and manually stitching context.

Our AI agents synthesise all of it: plan rules, cap table transactions, valuation reports, and more. Every query and action is grounded to your documents, not inferred. A deterministic ownership math that doesn't hallucinate.

Enterprise-grade security, built for AI.

Your documents stay encrypted and isolated to your account. Our agents work on them there, and what reaches a model is a stripped-down question — never your files.

How the AI works on your data, and what never leaves.

Built in, not plugged in.

Our agents run on our proprietary data model of your ownership, not a connector guessing at rows in your account. Nothing outside holds a key, and the figures come from your register.

Only the question crosses.

We work out the least needed to answer you — a short window of the conversation, and only the records your question touches. Your documents stay put.

Identities are stripped before it does.

Names, contact details, government and tax identifiers, and bank details are found and replaced with placeholders before the request leaves.

Nothing is kept, and nothing is learned.

It runs over an encrypted connection, inside an isolated environment we control. When the answer comes back the data is deleted — never stored, never read by a person, never used to train a model.

You ask

How many options does John Lennon have left to vest?

What leaves your account

How many options does <person_1> have left to vest?

Plus the vesting rows that question touches.

What never leaves

Who <person_1> actually is. Your documents. The register. Every other holder's row.

What we keep

Nothing. The answer comes back and the data is gone.
