Frequently asked questions.

Why do we need cap table management software?

There are dynamic changes in funding rounds, transactions, and stock option allocations. The cap table management software powers real-time transparent updates for all stakeholders. It ensures accuracy in recording ownership data, reducing the risk of errors often associated with manually updating spreadsheets.

Can EquityList help with valuation?

EquityList helps conduct valuation exercises powered by registered valuers and merchant bankers in a few clicks.

Can I view a historical version of my cap table in EquityList?

EquityList enables a comprehensive record of all your historical transactions, such as secondaries, conversions, and stock splits, and traces your cap table across different fundraising rounds.

Can we manage Convertible Notes / CCDs / SAFEs in EquityList?

EquityList allows you to manage multiple instruments used for fundraising, including convertibles like SAFE or Convertible Debt. We store the conversion terms in structured and unstructured forms so that companies know when the instruments will convert and the impacts.

Can we issue securities to our shareholders through EquityList?

You can issue securities to your shareholders by inviting them to your company dashboard.

Can we run hypothetical simulations on our cap table in EquityList?

Yes. EquityList helps execute various simulations for scenario modeling on its full-stack dashboard, allowing businesses to make better-informed decisions.
