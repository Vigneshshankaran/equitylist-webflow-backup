set-rounding-rules-that-match-your-legal-documents
New Feature
Compliance

🚀 Set rounding rules that match your legal documents

November 5, 2026

Under Compliance > Advanced Configuration, companies can now choose how fractions are rounded for option vesting, share certificate vesting and conversions into shares, so the platform's numbers match their scheme documents, grant letters and charters.

Your scheme documents, grant letters and charters say how fractions of a share should be rounded, and the rule can differ from document to document. Previously, EquityList used a fixed rounding rule for each calculation that companies couldn't change, so cap tables and vesting schedules could end up a few shares off from what the documents say.

  • Rounding modes: round down, round up or round to nearest, plus precise for option vesting.
  • Where leftover fractions go: 1,000 options vesting monthly over 48 months works out to 20.83 a month, but only whole options can vest. You can spread the extra across the schedule, so some months vest 21 and others 20 (40 months of 21 and 8 of 20). Or you can vest 20 every month and add everything left over to the last month, which then vests 60.
  • Converting into shares: for instruments such as preference shares or convertible notes, round each certificate on its own, or add up a holder's certificates in the same share class first and round once. With round down, two certificates that each convert to 1.6 shares give 2 shares when rounded separately (1 + 1), or 3 when added up first (3.2).

One setting applies across the company. A change applies only to calculations made after you save it. Vesting already scheduled on existing grants doesn't change: if a grant is due to vest 20 options on 1 January, it still vests 20 after you change the setting.

October 5, 2026

In a scheme's or grant's Exercise Period settings, a new "Until grant expiry date" option lets a leaver's post-termination exercise period run until each grant's own expiry date, rather than a fixed or tenure-based number of days.

Admins can choose it at the scheme or grant level, for stock option, SAR and RSU grants, in any of three places:
