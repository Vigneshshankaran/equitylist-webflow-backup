Frequently asked questions.

Is this list exhaustive?

No. These are 75 VCs we confirmed have made investments in Indian startups in 2025/26. The Indian VC landscape is large and constantly shifting. This is a curated starting point, not a complete directory.

How current is the contact info?

All contact details are sourced from publicly available information. We recommend verifying before outreach, as emails and handles can change over time.

What do I do with this list?

Use the stage focus and portfolio to find VCs who invest in companies like yours. Check their latest deals to confirm they're actively deploying. Then reach out using the contact details provided. A warm intro helps, but a sharp, well-researched cold email works too.

What should I have ready before reaching out?

At minimum, a pitch deck, your traction and key metrics, a clear ask (amount, round size, use of funds), and a clean cap table. VCs move faster when the fundamentals are already in order.
