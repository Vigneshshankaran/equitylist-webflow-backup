Frequently Asked Questions

Answering some common questions you might have

What is EquityList?

Equitylist is a full-stack equity and shareholder management platform for companies across India, Singapore, and the US. We help companies manage cap tables, stock options (ESOP, SAR, RSU, etc), data rooms, board management, and related compliances.

How is EquityList related to AngelList India?

EquityList was incepted and build within AngelList India as an internal project. We are part of the AngelList India family.

What businesses does EquityList support?

EquityList is designed for both listed and unlisted companies. Currently, our suite is available for companies in India, Singapore, and the United States, with a special focus on companies with dual-entity and cross-border structures.

How can startups use EquityList apart from cap table and stock options management?

As a full-stack equity and shareholder management software, our use cases go above and beyond cap table and stock options management, including:

  • Seek our in-house advisory services to create an ESOP, SAR, and other scheme document
  • Conduct valuation exercises for Indian (registered valuers/merchant banker) and US startups (409A)
  • Employee offer portal: beautifully relay the value of your ESOPs to future candidates at the time of joining
  • Investor updates and data rooms: seamlessly share confidential information with investors for audits and due diligence
  • Shareholder and board consent tools to seamlessly collect approvals on recurring notices and documents
  • End-to-end management of employee options exercise, surrenders, and buyback programs

Is EquityList available for free?

EquityList is free only for startups that need to manage less than 25 stakeholders and have raised less than $1Mn in funding. Find your most suitable pricing plan here.
