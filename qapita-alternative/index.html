How the AI works on your data, and what never leaves.

Built in, not plugged in.

Your Equity Agent runs inside EquityList itself, in an environment we control, not handed off to a separate AI product or plugin.

Only the question crosses.

A stripped-down question is what reaches a model. Your documents, your register, and every identity behind the question stay inside EquityList.

Identities are stripped before it does.

Names and identifiers are replaced with placeholders before a request ever leaves: a rule we can show you, not a policy that depends on which client you happened to connect.

Nothing is kept, and nothing is learned.

The answer comes back and the data is deleted. Nothing here is ever used to train a model.

Your documents stay inside EquityList, encrypted and isolated to your account. Our agent works on them there, and what reaches a model is a stripped-down question, never your files.

Questions teams askbefore they switch.

Does EquityList support publicly listed companies?

Yes. EquityList is built to manage both private and publicly listed companies. You can handle disclosures, trading windows, shareholder communications, and regulatory filings from the same platform, on the same register you built as a private company.

Qapita has AI too: what's actually different?

Qapita's AI investment spans a broader suite than just company equity. Your Equity Agent is built for one customer: the company running its own cap table and equity compensation. It reads your documents, enforces your plan rules, and works directly on your live register, not a static export.

Was Qapita affected by a data breach?

In December 2025, the ransomware group LockBit 5.0 publicly listed ESOP Direct, a Qapita company, as a claimed target and alleged it had exfiltrated data before encrypting systems.

How long does migrating from Qapita actually take?

A full migration can complete in as little as 12 hours once we have your data, with a dedicated point of contact throughout: your cap table, grants, and historical transactions imported end to end, not just a new empty account. Or sign up self-serve and complete onboarding yourself in minutes.

Do we have to re-execute our existing grants and agreements?

No. Migrating your cap table doesn't require re-papering anything. Your existing grants, agreements and consents move across as historical records, and everything new is issued on EquityList going forward.

Which regions does EquityList support?

EquityList is a global equity management platform supporting companies across the US, India, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Europe, with multi-jurisdictional compliance, reporting, and equity workflows on one register.
